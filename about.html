About CalcWise

CalcWise was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to clear, accurate financial tools without paying a dime or handing over their personal information.

Our Mission

Financial decisions—whether it's buying a home, taking out a loan, or planning for retirement—are among the most important choices you'll ever make. Yet too many people rely on back-of-the-envelope math or opaque tools from lenders who have a vested interest in the outcome.

We built CalcWise to be different. Our calculators are independent, transparent, and designed to give you the full picture—including every fee, every tax, and every dollar of interest. No sign-ups. No hidden agendas. Just the numbers you need to make confident decisions.

What We Offer

  • Free financial calculators — Mortgage, loan, auto loan, and more, all available instantly with no registration.
  • Expert educational content — In-depth guides and articles written to help you understand the "why" behind the numbers.
  • Unbiased comparisons — Side-by-side lender reviews and rate comparisons to help you find the best deals.

Our Approach

Every calculator on CalcWise uses standard, publicly documented financial formulas. We show our work in the content accompanying each tool so you can verify the math yourself. We don't store your inputs—all calculations happen in your browser, and your data never leaves your device.

How We Make Money

CalcWise is free to use and always will be. We support the site through display advertising and affiliate partnerships. When you click on a lender or product link and choose to open an account, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. This never influences our editorial content or calculator design—our recommendations are based on objective analysis, not compensation.

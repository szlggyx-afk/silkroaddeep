Urumqi Travel Guide

City Sightseeing

Urumqi serves as the main entry city for Xinjiang travel. You can explore local bazaars, museums and city parks within the urban area.

Local Food Culture

Plenty of traditional Xinjiang restaurants are distributed in downtown areas. Try local bread, noodles and grilled food.

Day Trips from Urumqi

Several natural scenic areas are reachable within one day’s drive. Early departure is recommended to save travel time.

Practical Tips

Prepare sun protection items. Drinking enough water is important due to dry climate. Plan accommodation close to public transport hubs.

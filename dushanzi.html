Dushanzi Travel Guide

Overview

Dushanzi is an important transportation hub on the northern route of the ancient Silk Road. Many self-driving travelers use Dushanzi as the starting point for mountain road trips.

Transportation & Road Tips

Highway access is convenient. Pay attention to seasonal weather changes. Some mountain sections may have strong winds and temperature differences.

Local Food & Accommodation

You can find affordable hotels and local Xinjiang restaurants near the city center. It is recommended to book accommodation in advance during the travel peak season.

Travel Suggestions

The best travel window is from late spring to early autumn. Prepare warm clothing even in summer for mountain excursions.
